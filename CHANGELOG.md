# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Four boss roads between the worlds, each one several screens tall,
  the view rolling up after him as he climbs: The Long Haul, with a
  steamroller grinding up behind; The Thaw, with the meltwater rising;
  The Long Night, with a cloud of crows sweeping in; and The Dragon
  Wakes, with the dragon himself on his heels
- Resting banks halfway up a boss road: reach one and a fall starts him
  again from there, with time on the clock topped up
- A best time for every boss road, shown on its card
- Cameos from the rest of Asciiverse: Westering wagons, Panzer toy
  tanks, Sleighride sleighs, the Saalis plane in the sky, a Lunar
  Descent lander at night, Marble Recovery marbles, uXo bombs,
  Hammurabi's rats and a 1D-Pong ball, and four new kisses to go with
  them
- A goldfish in the Quiet Pond who offers a wish
- A saved game from before keeps its place in the story

## 1.0.0

- The endless road keeps a record for every world: the quickest crossing
  on the village lanes, the highway, the river, the winter road, the night
  road and the castle, shown as you cross and in the high-score table
- Rain on Moon Puddle: a hop onto the wet road slides one further
- Kinder fast lanes where the step up was too steep: the bicycles on
  Pasture Gate, the sleds on Mitten Pass and the dragon's fire on the
  Dragon's Doorstep
- Played from the first road through the pond to the last page of the
  finale, again and again, by a player that hops itself

## 0.9.0

- Whatever waits on the far side stands tall against the sky, so you can
  see the mailbox, the cow or the duchess before you get there
- A drawbridge over the moat that goes up now and then
- Market stalls on Market Road
- Rain on the endless road now and then: a hop onto a wet road slides
  one further
- A line for whatever flattened him: the goose always wins
- World cards show the way so far, a heart for every kiss
- The finale moves: the kiss that changes nothing, half a crown each,
  and at last a prince

## 0.8.0

- Options on the title: the pace (normal, gentle at three quarters speed,
  or slow at half speed), lives (5, 9, or never run out), sound (on,
  quiet or off) and your own keys for the hops
- The pace can be changed from the pause menu too
- High-score tables for the story, the endless road and today's road,
  with your name and how far you got; scores made with gentler settings
  are marked
- Kiss scenes: a moment of hope as sparkles gather round him, then they
  fizzle out; the last word on every kiss; a tally of kisses and princes
- The first highway crossings are a little kinder after the village

## 0.7.0

- Fog on the endless road now and then: only the lanes near you can be
  seen
- Old planks in the water to rest on, on the endless road
- The demo never touches your save
- The title fits every item at 80x24

## 0.6.0

- Railway crossings: a train comes through now and then, with a bell and
  blinking lights before it (the Desert Stretch, and on the endless road)
- Flies land now and then; eat one for 200 points
- Today's road: an endless road that is the same for everyone today, with
  its own best score
- Two players together: both toads on the endless road, and the road is
  done only when both are across
- Livelier kiss scenes: the cow licks him shiny, the cactus says ow, the
  duchess screams, the dragon puffs smoke, and he leaves the heron quickly
- A little tune of its own for every world
- Tighter clocks on every crossing

## 0.5.0

- The whole story: a prince turned toad crosses eighteen roads, rivers,
  icy lanes, night roads and a castle moat, kissing whatever waits on the
  other side, until the quiet pond and the princess
- Seven worlds, each with its own traffic, sky and kiss
- The pond: lily pads that sink and drift, diagonal hops, and a princess
  who hops away
- The endless road, harder the further you go
- Two players racing at one keyboard, or with two gamepads
- Sound made in code, a croak for every hop
- Gamepad support
