# Changelog

## 0.8.0

- Options on the title: the pace (normal, gentle at three quarters speed,
  or slow at half speed), lives (5, 9, or never run out), sound (on,
  quiet or off) and your own keys for the hops
- The pace can be changed from the pause menu too
- High-score tables for the story, the endless road and today's road,
  with your name and how far you got; scores made with gentler settings
  are marked
- Kiss scenes: a moment of hope as sparkles gather round him, then they
  fizzle out; the last word on every kiss; a tally of kisses and princes
- The first highway crossings are a little kinder after the village

## 0.7.0

- Fog on the endless road now and then: only the lanes near you can be
  seen
- Old planks in the water to rest on, on the endless road
- The demo never touches your save
- The title fits every item at 80x24

## 0.6.0

- Railway crossings: a train comes through now and then, with a bell and
  blinking lights before it (the Desert Stretch, and on the endless road)
- Flies land now and then; eat one for 200 points
- Today's road: an endless road that is the same for everyone today, with
  its own best score
- Two players together: both toads on the endless road, and the road is
  done only when both are across
- Livelier kiss scenes: the cow licks him shiny, the cactus says ow, the
  duchess screams, the dragon puffs smoke, and he leaves the heron quickly
- A little tune of its own for every world
- Tighter clocks on every crossing

## 0.5.0

- The whole story: a prince turned toad crosses eighteen roads, rivers,
  icy lanes, night roads and a castle moat, kissing whatever waits on the
  other side, until the quiet pond and the princess
- Seven worlds, each with its own traffic, sky and kiss
- The pond: lily pads that sink and drift, diagonal hops, and a princess
  who hops away
- The endless road, harder the further you go
- Two players racing at one keyboard, or with two gamepads
- Sound made in code, a croak for every hop
- Gamepad support
