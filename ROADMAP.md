# Prince Toad roadmap

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-14)

The whole story, first road to the pond: seven worlds, eighteen crossings
and kisses, the Quiet Pond with sinking lily pads, diagonals and the
princess, the finale. Endless road and a two-player race. Sound in code,
gamepad, save, demo.

## Towards 1.0

- **Played by people and tuned by feel:** speeds, gaps and clocks per
  crossing, the pond's sinking time, lives.
- **More world variety:** a bridge crossing, a market day with stalls, a
  railway crossing with a gate and a train, fog, rain that makes roads
  slick; things that come and go, like a fly to eat for points.
- **Endless:** daily road with the same seed for everyone, a record per
  world.
- **Two players:** a co-op mode where both must cross, one toad per pad.
- **Scenes:** each kiss a little animated (the cow's lick, the cactus
  spines, the duchess's scream shaking the box).
- **Music:** a short tune per world, made in code.
